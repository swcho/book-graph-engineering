Educational infographic, clean flat design, 16:9 landscape, soft off-white background, generous whitespace, modern sans-serif typography.

Title at top center: "Expiration vs Correction" with small Korean subtitle "만료 vs 정정".

Layout: two large side-by-side comparison panels below the title, plus a narrow warning strip along the bottom.

LEFT PANEL (blue accent, rounded card) — header "EXPIRATION (만료)" with a calendar-flip icon. Tagline beneath header: "True then, not now". A horizontal timeline from January to June: a solid blue bar labeled "Grade A" spanning Jan to May, then a green bar labeled "Grade B" from May onward, with a small scissors icon cutting the bar at May and caption "reality changed". Below the timeline a small query chip: "March? → A".

RIGHT PANEL (orange accent, rounded card) — header "CORRECTION (정정)" with a pencil-eraser icon. Tagline beneath header: "Wrong even then". A horizontal timeline from January to June: a crossed-out red bar labeled "Grade A (typo)" spanning the whole period, and beneath it a green bar labeled "Grade B" also spanning the whole period, with an arrow from red bar to green bar labeled "fixed in May". Below the timeline a small query chip: "March? → B".

Between the two panels, a bold "VS" badge in a circle.

BOTTOM STRIP (light red background, warning triangle icon): left text "Mix them up → past reports rewrite themselves"; right text with a small speech-bubble icon: "Ask at input: changed or mistyped?".

Style: flat vector illustration, crisp thin outlines, blue/orange/green/red accent palette, short labels only, no long sentences, high text legibility.
